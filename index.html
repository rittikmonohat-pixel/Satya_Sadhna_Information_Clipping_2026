02 · About

About Satya Sadhna.

Discover Satya Sadhna, a millennia-old meditation technique by Acharya Shri Jin Chandra Suriji. Find inner peace, self-discovery, and humanitarian values. Experience present-moment awareness and aim for enlightenment.

I.

Acharya Ji

Acharya Shri Jin Chandra Suriji Maharajsahab is the successor to the ‘Khartargach’ tradition of Lord Mahavir, the 24th Tirthankar of the Jain religion. He started practicing meditation from the initial days of his Acharyapad and felt Satya Sadhna deserved to be spread to a larger section of society due to its immense benefits.

II.

Process

Satya Sadhna is taught through an exclusive 10-day course, during which participants must live in isolation, with no contact with the outside world. It offers a structured & disciplined approach to meditation, with the goal of discovering one’s true self and leading a happy, balanced, and peaceful life.

III.

Benefits

Satya Sadhna helps achieve equanimity, focus, compassion, confidence, inner peace, humanitarian values, reduced stress, improved relationships, and overall well-being. It is a transformative meditation practice that guides people on the path to enlightenment.

04 · The Path

From a first sitting to a lifelong practice.

The path unfolds naturally for those who continue.

1. The 10-Day Course

The foundation. A residential, silent course where the practice is taught from the ground up.

2. Daily Practice

After the course, the practice continues at home — twice daily. The seed is planted; tending it is the work of years.

3. Recurring Courses

Weekly Sunday sittings at the Kendras, plus monthly anchors in other cities. The rhythm for old students.

4. Deepen Through Service

Serve at courses, support new applicants, or contribute to the Kendra. Service is itself a practice.
